# SDSG Undertale: Mahdi Alt Music Webport

A **web port of Undertale for SDSG**, but with all music replaced by my **Mahdi Alt tracks**.  
Play Undertale in your browser with a fresh audio twist.

## What This Is

- Full Undertale gameplay in-browser (via SDSG)  
- Original music replaced with **Mahdi Alt versions**  
- Works on PC, mobile, or any platform that runs modern browsers  
- No downloads required — just open the site and play

This lets you experience Undertale with **my custom soundtrack** without installing anything.
